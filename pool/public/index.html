How to Play & Install

What is Snooker Tubey?

Pool Subway is a twist on traditional 8-ball pool, combining cue skill with geographic knowledge of New York's iconic subway network. Players must call out a valid NY Subway station that corresponds with the colour and type (solid/stripe) of the ball potted.

Basic Rules

  • Play standard 8-ball pool. Solids map to primary lines, stripes map to secondary lines.
  • After potting a ball, call out a NY Subway station matching the ball's color mapping.
  • You have 10 seconds to call a station.

Colour-to-Line Assignments

  • Yellow: (Solid: N, Q) | (Stripe: R, W)
  • Blue: (Solid: A) | (Stripe: C, E)
  • Red: (Solid: 1, 2) | (Stripe: 3)
  • Purple: (Solid: 7) | (Stripe: 7)
  • Orange: (Solid: B, D) | (Stripe: F, M)
  • Green: (Solid: 4) | (Stripe: 5, 6)
  • Brown/Burgundy: (Solid: J) | (Stripe: Z)
  • Black (8-Ball): L, G

Stations serving multiple lines can count for any of their associated lines.

How to Install the App

You can install this tracker on your device for offline use!

  • iOS (Safari): Tap the Share button at the bottom, then scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome): Tap the three-dot menu icon in the top right, then tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  • Desktop: Look for the install icon in the URL bar of your browser.
